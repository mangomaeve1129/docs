## Overview

Equippable civilian grade bio-monitors, broadcasting alerts over the emergergency channel in the event of user danger or death. 

## Background

When I am playing paramedic, my most dreaded situation is salvage deciding they can take on the vroid, dying, and leaving no-one the wiser about position or even that they've died at all. The bio-monitor is my solution to this problem, acting as a sort of civillian grade death rattler implant.

## Features to be added

A basic biological monitor that can be holstered on any main clothing item(Jumpsuits, Jumpskirts, etc.) in the same vein as the detective's revolver and shoulder holster. Ideally I'd like to expand this later to include department specific monitors that can be researched and manufactured to give more details or otherwise supplement that role (Example: An engineer goes critical working on the singularity, and their specialized engineering monitor alerts emergency about the nearest beacon (or exact coordinates if they enable that setting on it), and hazardous local radiation levels).
